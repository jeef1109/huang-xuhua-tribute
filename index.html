A LIFE OF DEDICATION

Huang Xuhua

The Father of China's Nuclear Submarines

Huang Xuhua, a great scientist in China, was born in 1926 and passed away in 2025. He is widely known as “the Father of China’s Nuclear Submarines”.

He was a real hero who made many contributions to our country. In 1958, he joined a research team to design China’s first nuclear submarine. As the chief designer, he and his team faced countless difficulties, but he never gave up. After years of hard work, he helped launch China’s first nuclear submarine in 1970.

For his outstanding achievements, he was awarded the Medal of the Republic in 2019. He dedicated himself to our country’s national defense. Thanks to his hard work, China’s nuclear submarines made significant improvements. His spirit also encourages all of us. We will always respect and remember this great scientist.
